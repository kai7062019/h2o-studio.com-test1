# H2O Studio Design Inspiration - Havas Style

## Key Design Elements from Havas

1. **Color Palette**: Black background with red/bright accents and white text
2. **Typography**: Bold, large sans-serif headlines (Havas uses custom/geometric fonts)
3. **Layout**: Minimalist, asymmetric, diagonal lines and geometric elements
4. **Navigation**: Clean, minimal top navigation with language/investor links
5. **Hero Section**: Large bold text, dark background, geometric accents
6. **Visual Elements**: Diagonal lines, geometric shapes, neon accents (green, yellow)
7. **Spacing**: Generous whitespace, breathing room
8. **Premium Feel**: Sophisticated, high-end creative agency aesthetic

## H2O Studio Adaptation

- Black and white as primary colors
- Minimal accent colors (possibly subtle neon for Gen Z appeal)
- Clean, geometric typography
- Diagonal lines and asymmetric layouts
- Minimalist component design
- Premium, sophisticated feel while staying relevant to Gen Z
