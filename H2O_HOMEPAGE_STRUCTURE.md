## Design Characteristics

1. **Layout**: Alternating text (right) + full-width images (below)
2. **Color Scheme**: Black background, white text
3. **Typography**: Large, bold headlines
4. **Images**: Full-width, high-quality photography with cinematic feel
5. **Pacing**: Slow scroll, contemplative messaging
6. **Call to Action**: "Contact us" link at bottom

## Key Messages to Incorporate

1. Unlearn. Reimagine. Empower.
2. We don't follow trends. We create markets.
3. Between brands and creators, we build invisible bridges
4. Creators inspire, transform, and elevate
5. Creativity meets commerce
6. Brands seeking relevance, creators seeking resonance
